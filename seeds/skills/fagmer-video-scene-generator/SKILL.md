# Fagmer Video — Scene Generator

**Input:** validated `script.json`. **Output:** scene text and visual choices in `script.json`
(before validation) and title-card assets via `ace capture`.
**Hard stop:** any asset without license and provenance; stock or third-party art without a
license that allows commercial social use; generated visuals presented as product proof.

## Steps

1. For each non-capture scene, the Remotion template draws the card from `onScreenText` and the
   scene kind: hook = large kinetic line; problem = pain statement; rubric/evidence/verdict =
   scoreboard (AI Battle); overview = node-and-arrow card (Automation Blueprint); cta = profile
   link card. Keep `onScreenText` to 6 words or fewer, high contrast, no claims beyond the
   narration.
2. Art direction is fixed by the brand: dark navy `#0B1426`, teal accent `#2EC4B6`, white text,
   captions inside the TikTok safe zone (top 180 px, bottom 420 px, right 140 px clear).
3. Only original title cards (generated by `ace capture`, license `original`) in R1. If the user
   wants stock or AI imagery, ask by click, record its source URL and license in the scene's
   `visual`, and never use it as evidence that a product works.
4. After `ace capture`, review `assets.json`: every scene has an asset, license and provenance.

## Production guide

Follow `docs/production-guide.md` sections 1 and 3. For this stage:

- Show the real interface, not a metaphor for it; cards support the story, never replace it.
- Colour is dramaturgy: the accent marks the one word or region that matters, not decoration.
- Explanation labels accumulate and stay; they do not flash in and out.
- Avoid the same card mould in every scene: vary the layout with the story beat.
- Check stills before the full render: text on text, boxes misaligned, elements out of frame.
